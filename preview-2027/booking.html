The band

Formed by guitarist, songwriter and producer Niall Hone, formerly of Hawkwind, Lords of Form blend space rock, heavy psychedelia and analogue electronics with the atmosphere and folklore of the Sussex landscape. Their latest album, Fallow & Fain, brings that sound together with vocalist and guitarist Tony Whiting.

For fans of Hawkwind, King Buffalo, Elder, Earthless, Ozric Tentacles and Ash Ra Tempel.

Live experience

Recent Hawkwind support appearances include The Hexagon, Reading; Bradford Live; and Morecambe Winter Gardens for Hawkwind Friends & Relations.
